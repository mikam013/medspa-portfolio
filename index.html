AI automation

An AI receptionist that never misses a lead

Calls get missed every day while your team is with clients. Every missed call and website chat gets an instant, friendly text that answers questions from your real price list and books into your calendar.

  1. 1Missed call or new lead → text sent in under 10 seconds
  2. 2AI answers questions using your prices, policies and FAQs
  3. 3Offers real open times from your calendar and books them
  4. 4Reminders the day before and 2 hours before
  5. 5Hands the chat to your team for medical questions

Built in GoHighLevel with your approved answers. It never gives medical advice and always lets a person take over.

AI automation · follow-up system

Six automations running 24/7

Set up once in GoHighLevel, tailored to your treatments and voice. Your team only steps in when a real person is needed.

New lead

When: Form, ad or quiz lead
  1. Text + email in 60 seconds
  2. AI answers questions
  3. Books or tags for a call

Instagram DM

When: Comment a keyword on a Reel
  1. Auto-DM with guide or price
  2. Follow-up if no reply
  3. Lead saved in CRM

No-show

When: Appointment marked missed
  1. Friendly “we missed you” text
  2. Rebooking link
  3. Team task if no reply

Reviews

When: Visit completed
  1. Thank-you text 2 hours later
  2. Happy → Google review link
  3. Unhappy → private note to owner

Rebooking

When: 12 weeks after Botox
  1. “Time for your touch-up?” text
  2. Member price reminder
  3. One-tap booking

Win-back

When: No visit in 6 months
  1. Personal check-in message
  2. Seasonal offer
  3. Moves to newsletter if quiet
